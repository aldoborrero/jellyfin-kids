Kids

Mark the Jellyfin users who are kids. Kids get the kids mode of clients such as Peke for Jellyfin, with the settings you choose here.

Limits only apply in the kids app. Other apps keep playing when a kid's time is up, the sitting limit is reached or allowed hours end.

Users

Tick Kid for each child, then open their settings for screen time and the home screen. Each kid shows what still leaves them unprotected, with a link to fix it in Jellyfin.

Kid profiles, the parent code, screen time and channels are kept in this folder on the server. Back it up with Jellyfin's data; Jellyfin's own backup does not include it. Don't delete it: that loses every kid's settings.
